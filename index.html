Welcome to CodeLab!

Join our school club to unlock the power of technology! CodeLab is your gateway to mastering efficient PC use and diving into the exciting world of coding. Whether you're a beginner or an enthusiast, we’re here to help you learn and grow.

What We Teach

At CodeLab, we cover essential skills for using your PC effectively, from file management to productivity tools. Plus, we teach coding in popular languages like Python, JavaScript, C++, HTML, and CSS—building everything from websites to games and apps!

Why Join CodeLab?

Be part of a fun, collaborative community where you’ll gain hands-on experience, work on real projects, and prepare for a tech-driven future. CodeLab equips you with skills for school, hobbies, and even future careers in technology!

What Will We Teach Students?

Everything we know ourselves, starting from the basics of programming, Discord and Telegram bots, databases, Blender, websites, different programming languages, and we'll even teach how to make video games! The more people participate, the better, because we can create large projects with 10+ people. The projects themselves will greatly help in programming skills and teamwork.
